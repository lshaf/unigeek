# UniGeek 1.9.0
*2026-11-01*

The biggest NFC/RFID release yet: a unified toolkit spanning PN532, Chameleon Ultra, and the new ST25R3916 backend, with full MIFARE Classic key recovery and experimental EMV reading. Plus a LAN pentest suite — network scanners, remote-access clients, and ARP-spoofing MITM — and new WiFi/BLE monitoring tools.

## New Features
> **Unified NFC/RFID Tools**
> One HF/LF toolkit across PN532, Chameleon Ultra, and ST25R3916 — dump read/write/edit, NDEF tools, identity tools, a memory viewer, and UID-to-tag writing, all with a harmonized workflow.

> **ST25R3916 reader support**
> New HF/LF reader backend with MIFARE Classic, Ultralight/NTAG, and experimental Type 4A/4B/DESFire/FeliCa support.

> **MIFARE Classic key recovery**
> MFKey32, Darkside, Hard Nested, Backdoor SEN, and dictionary attacks, chained into a Full Recovery flow with a shared discovered-key store.

> **EMV card reading (PN532)**
> Experimental EMV application discovery and APDU diagnostics.

> **Network Scanners**
> Discover hosts and services on the LAN: web servers, remote shells, file services, IoT/media devices, printers, plus SSDP and mDNS discovery.

> **Remote Access clients**
> SSH, SFTP, FTP, Telnet, raw TCP, and WebDAV clients with terminal hotkeys and host-key verification.

> **MITM with ARP spoofing**
> Subnet ARP poisoning with real packet forwarding and PCAP capture to SD (Wireshark-ready), under WiFi → Network → Attacks.

> **DHCP Attack**
> Starve the router's DHCP pool and serve rogue leases naming the device as gateway and DNS.

> **WiFi & BLE monitoring**
> Packet Monitor, Packet Sniffer (PCAP), WiFi/BLE Watchcat activity tracking, and a BLE Watchdog.

> **WiFi & BLE Fox Hunt**
> RSSI-guided device hunting with a thermal color scale.

> **Unigotchi**
> Pwnagotchi-style automatic handshake hunter with a mood face, launched from the EAPOL Capture menu.

> **Sub-GHz Brute Force**
> Fixed-code key sweep over the RMT peripheral across nine protocols (CAME, NICE, FAAC, Princeton, and more).

> **T-Embed virtual HID keyboard**
> On-device virtual keyboard with latched-modifier display over USB/BLE HID.

> **Web App Store**
> Browse and install Lua apps directly over USB/BLE from the web tools.

## Improvements
- **Reorganized menus** — WiFi, BLE, and Network menus regrouped into intent-based submenus (Scanners, Attacks, Pranks, Services, Internet).
- **Standardized UI messages** across the firmware, with cleaned-up status/progress rendering.
- **Keyboard layout setting** — pick a full or classic 12-key input layout.
- **Sub-GHz jammer modes** — Full Power, Intermittent, Noise Storm, and Freq Sweep.
- **NRF24 jammer rework** — constant-carrier channel hopper; fixed MouseJack ESB injection.
- **Unified BLE Spam engine** with a working advertising path.
- **Active Karma attack engine** with captive-portal memory-leak fix.
- **TV-B-Gone** — universal multi-device power-off codes and a faster sweep.
- **Configurable CC1101** RF-switch and antenna-switch pins.
- **Cardputer power** — light sleep and a power menu on M5 Cardputer / Cardputer ADV.
- **Larger app partitions** on 8/16 MB boards; NFC/RFID modules gated off 4 MB boards to fit.
- **Lua list** shows script titles instead of filenames.

## Bug Fixes
- **Watchdog** — cap tracked MAC/SSID maps to stop an OOM crash.
- **EAPOL** — reject handshake pairs with mismatched replay counters.
- **Captive portal** — DNS-spoof probes now redirect like Evil Twin's.
- **T-Embed** — fix battery SOC register, SubGHz and encoder regressions, PN532 I2C detection.
- **Remote shells** — FTP/SFTP Back no longer loops at the root directory.
